# Sift privacy policy

_Last updated: November 20, 2026_

Sift is an iPhone app that shows the Instagram and YouTube mobile websites with their feeds filtered out.

**Sift collects nothing.** It has no account, no server, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

- **Your logins** go straight to Instagram, Meta or Google in the app's built-in browser. Sift never sees or stores your password. The sign-in cookies stay on your phone, the same way Safari keeps them.
- **Your settings** (which features are on) are saved only on your phone.
- **The Reels and Shorts list** that powers "Following only" mode is a list of video IDs from pages you opened. It is saved only on your phone and deleted after 7 days.
- **Instagram and YouTube** still see what you do on their sites, under their own privacy policies.
- **Crash reports and feedback** you choose to send through TestFlight go to the developer through Apple.

To erase everything Sift keeps, use **Settings, Sign out of everything** in the app, or delete the app.

Questions: use **Send Beta Feedback** in the TestFlight app.
